What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on your device when you visit it. They can help a website recognize a browser or remember certain information between visits.

Depending on their purpose, cookies may support essential website functions, remember preferences, measure website performance, or support advertising and marketing activities.

Cookies do not all work in the same way. Some are temporary and are removed when you close your browser, while others may remain on your device for a defined period or until you delete them.

Types of Cookies

Cookies are commonly grouped according to their purpose. The following categories explain how they may function on a website.

Essential Cookies

These cookies may be necessary for core website operations, security, or features that you request. Disabling them may prevent some website functions from working correctly.

Preference Cookies

These cookies may remember choices such as language or other interface preferences so the website can provide a more consistent experience.

Analytics Cookies

If analytics tools are enabled, these cookies may help measure visits, understand how visitors navigate pages, and identify areas where the website could be improved.

Marketing Cookies

If marketing technologies are enabled, these cookies may help measure advertising performance or support advertising tailored to a visitor's interests.

The categories actually used by PolicyPath should be confirmed through an audit of its installed scripts, plugins, and third-party integrations.

How to Manage Cookies

Most browsers allow you to view, block, restrict, or delete cookies through their privacy or security settings. The available controls depend on your browser and device.

You may be able to:

  • Delete cookies already stored on your device.
  • Block cookies from specific websites.
  • Restrict third-party cookies.
  • Configure your browser to notify you when cookies are set.

Please note that blocking or deleting certain cookies may affect website functionality or cause preferences to be lost.

If PolicyPath provides a cookie preference or consent tool, you may also use that tool to manage the categories of cookies covered by its settings.
