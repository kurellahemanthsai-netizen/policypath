YOUR CLAIMS JOURNEY

How the Claims Process Works

From the first notification to the final decision, understand each step of your claims journey.

Guidance at Every Step

Know what to expect throughout your claim.

01

Report Your Claim

Notify your insurance provider and share the essential details of the incident or loss.

02

Submit Required Documents

Provide the forms, supporting documents, and evidence requested by your insurer.

03

Review and Assessment

Your insurer evaluates the information and may request further details or verification.

04

Decision and Settlement

Receive the claim decision and any applicable settlement details or next-step instructions.

Processes and timelines vary according to your insurer, policy terms, and claim type.

PREPARE WITH CONFIDENCE

Documents You May Need

Having the right documents ready can help make your claims experience smoother. Requirements vary by claim type and insurance provider.

01

Health Claim

Keep relevant medical and hospitalization records ready for your insurer.

  • Medical bills and receipts
  • Discharge summary and reports
  • Policy and identity details
Health claim details
02

Life Claim

Beneficiaries may need to provide policy information and supporting records.

  • Policy documents
  • Claimant identity details
  • Death certificate, when applicable
Life claim details
03

Motor Claim

Collect the vehicle, incident, and damage details requested by your insurer.

  • Driving licence and vehicle RC
  • Policy details and incident photos
  • FIR, where required
Motor claim details
04

Property Claim

Document the damage or loss and retain relevant ownership and expense records.

  • Property and policy details
  • Photos or videos of the damage
  • Repair estimates and invoices
Property claim details

This is a general checklist, not a complete document list. Your insurer may request additional documents depending on your policy and circumstances.

From Claim to Resolution

Understand each stage of your insurance claim, from reporting the incident to receiving your insurer's decision.

Review & Assessment
